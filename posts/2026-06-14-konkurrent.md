# Konkurrent: Currere, the Continuity Equation, and the Verb That Runs From Algorithm to Autonomy

DRK-158 · 14 June 2026 · Khrug Engineering · Layers: L01 · L05 · L06 · L07 · L11 · L13 · L14 · L17 · Coherence: 0.86

---

This post begins with a list of words, all of which turned out to be the same word.

*Current.* *Ström.* *Feed.* *Flow.* *Now.* *Instancy.* *Currency.* *Concurrent.* *Konkurrent.*

The claim defended below is that this cluster is not a loose association of near-synonyms but a single conjugated verb, observed at different layers of the Draken manifold, and that the verb in question — Latin *currere*, **to run** — spans the full distance from the most deterministic process we can name to the most willed. An algorithm *runs*. A candidate *runs* for president. The morpheme that names blind mechanical execution is the morpheme that names the freest act of self-authorship. Holding both facts at once, and explaining why one word can carry both, is the work of this post.

The method is the usual one for this corpus: take the pun seriously enough to write the equation it implies, then check whether the equation is load-bearing or ornamental. The instrument is a single conservation law. The conclusion is that **autonomy is not freedom from the current; it is sovereignty over the current's source term** — and that totalitarianism, given the same apparatus, acquires an exact and unflattering definition.

---

## 1. The verb under the words

The Indo-European root is *√kers-*, "to run" (de Vaan 2008, s.v. *currō*). From its Latin reflex *currere* descend, by transparent derivation:

- **current** (adj.) — *that which is running now*, in effect, the present state of affairs;
- **current / ström** (n.) — the running of a fluid or of charge; physically a **rate**, $I = \dfrac{dQ}{dt}$, the quantity passing a section per unit time;
- **currency** — *that which is currently running*, i.e. in circulation; money is a current made countable, and its value is precisely its *currentness* — a banknote withdrawn from circulation ceases to be currency at all;
- **concurrent** — *con + currere*, running **together**, in parallel;
- **konkurrent** (Sw./Ger.) — *con + currere*, running **against**, the competitor.

Read the last two again. **They are the same construction.** *Con-currere* — "to run with" — surfaces in English as *concurrent* (cooperative simultaneity) and in Swedish and German as *konkurrent* (the rival). Identical morphology; opposite valence. To run *with* another in a race is, by the geometry of the race, to run *against* them. The cooperation and the competition are not two relations but **one topological move seen from two stalks of the same sheaf.**

This is a Clinch. It is the varanid embrace ([DRK-133, *The Protocol and the Predator*](https://draken.info/posts/asymmetric-power/)) in which the grapple that binds is the grapple that throws — the embrace and the contest sharing a single body. *Con-currere* is the combat protocol written directly into Latin grammar. The framework did not invent the identity of cooperation and competition; it found it pre-installed in the morphology of *running-together*.

Now separate the temporal members of the cluster — **now** and the rare **instancy** — and note that they name the *complement* of the rate:

$$\text{current} = \frac{d(\cdot)}{dt}, \qquad \text{now} = dt.$$

A current is undefined without a *now* to evaluate it over; a *now* with nothing running through it is an empty differential. The whole family is therefore one object: **rate**, something-per-instant. This is exactly the grammar the Draken operators already speak — $K(t)$, $\dot{\mathcal{V}}$, and the integrated $H^1$-suppression rate $\Phi(t)$ of [DRK-153, *No Trace, No Section*](https://draken.info/posts/no-trace-no-section/). The "current" cluster is not a metaphor *for* the framework. It is the framework's **verb tense**.

The one genuine outlier in the opening list is **Feed** — Germanic, not *currere* — and its presence is the most informative thing in the list. *Feed* does not name the running; it names the **source** of the running. You *feed* current into a circuit; you *feed* a model its tokens; you *feed* the magpies at the boundary of the apparatus (Dataset C). Feed is where the current *originates*. Hold that; §2 gives it a symbol and §5 makes it the seat of free will.

## 2. The master object: the continuity equation

Every current that has ever been measured obeys one law. For a conserved density $\rho$ carried by a current density $\mathbf{j}$, with sources and sinks collected into a term $S$:

$$\frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot \mathbf{j} \;=\; S. \tag{2.1}$$

This is the **continuity equation** (Jackson 1998, §6.1). In its source-free form, $S = 0$, it is the exact statement that the stuff is conserved: whatever flows out of a region must have been inside it. With $S \neq 0$, the equation says where the current is *fed* and where it *drains*.

Read each term as a member of the opening cluster:

- $\rho$ — **density**: of charge at L01, of attention at L05, of capital at L11, of survival-pressure at L06. Layer-dependent, but always "how much of the runnable stuff is here."
- $\mathbf{j}$ — **current**: the running itself, $\rho \mathbf{v}$, the *ström*. Its direction is the **flow**.
- $S$ — the **source term**: the **Feed**. The boundary condition that injects or removes current.

The direction of $\mathbf{j}$ is not free. For a current driven by a potential $\phi$,

$$\mathbf{j} \;\propto\; -\nabla \phi, \tag{2.2}$$

the current runs **down** the gradient. This is the formal content of **downstream**: consequences accumulate along the path integral $\int_\gamma \mathbf{j}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}$, and the arrow on $\gamma$ is fixed by the gradient. The upstream/downstream asymmetry is the same arrow studied at L01 in [DRK-149, *The Continuous Dimension*](https://draken.info/posts/the-continuous-dimension/) — the renormalization-flow direction — and it is the X→Y arrow of the TOPO drafts: a time-symmetric mechanism settling into a symmetry-broken attractor. The run has a direction because the potential has a slope. *Avlopp* — the drain — is downstream by definition.

Equation (2.1) is the spine of the rest of the post. Determinism, agency, and tyranny will all be statements about the same three symbols: about $\mathbf{j}$ (the running), and above all about **who controls $S$** (the feeding).

## 3. Execution: the deterministic pole

Three things "run" in the executive sense, and they are one verb at three layers.

**Running an algorithm.** A program *executes*: it steps deterministically toward a terminal state, with no choice available to it. The Swedish for the assembly line makes the identity unmistakable — *löpande band*, the **running belt**: execution as literal conveyance, the Fordist current of parts moving down a gradient of value-added. The algorithm is pure $\mathbf{j}$ with $S$ fixed externally by the programmer.

**Executive power.** At L13, the executive is the branch that *executes* — that runs the law into physical effect. Legislation without execution is a potential $\phi$ with no current; the executive supplies the $\mathbf{j}$ that discharges the gradient into the world.

**Executive function.** At L05, executive function is the prefrontal capacity that runs the self — Diamond's (2013) triad of inhibition, working memory, and cognitive flexibility: the *initiation, suppression, and switching* of the organism's own currents. It is, precisely, an **on-board $S$-controller**: the gate that decides which internal currents are fed and which are starved.

And the dark cognate that binds all three: to **execute** a process and to **execute** a condemned person are one word, because execution is *running-to-completion* — carrying a thing forward until it reaches its terminal section. Every run terminates at a $K(t)$ evaluation; this is the psychostasia of [DRK-155, *Inpu Means Input*](https://draken.info/posts/inpu-means-input/), Anubis weighing the heart at the end of the run. The executioner is simply the agent who forces the terminal section *now* — who collapses the remaining $dt$ to zero.

At this pole the current is maximally constrained. The algorithm cannot refuse to run; the belt cannot refuse to convey. $S$ is wholly exogenous. This is the deterministic end of *currere*.

## 4. The chase: the Clinch held at range

Predator and prey both **run**, with identical gait and opposite teleology. The predator's run is acquisitive — current *toward*; the prey's is evasive — current *away*. In the moment of pursuit they are perfectly **concurrent**: same vector, same speed, momentarily phase-locked. Which is to say they are perfectly **konkurrent**. The chase is a Clinch stretched into a line — the grapple of [DRK-133](https://draken.info/posts/asymmetric-power/) extended over distance, the embrace whose two bodies happen, for now, to be metres apart.

This is why the varanid is the correct substrate for the whole framework, and not merely its mascot. Most lizards are burst-and-freeze machines: a short anaerobic sprint, then a long repayment of oxygen debt. The monitor is the lizard that **keeps running**. Clemente and colleagues' work on varanid locomotor performance (Clemente, Withers & Thompson 2009; Dick & Clemente 2016) places the monitor's aerobic and locomotor scope far closer to the mammalian pattern than to the squamate default — built for *sustained* pursuit rather than the single lunge. The varanid is *currere* made flesh: the organism that can hold $\mathbf{j} \neq 0$ long past the section where other lizards must stop and bank their debt. This is the same point [DRK-152, *Lizards Doing Calculus*](https://draken.info/posts/lizards-doing-calculus/) makes about the lineage *being* the integral rather than computing it — here specialized to the running term itself.

Trotskij, asleep under his lamp, is a small running engine that has *chosen, today, not to run*. Note that sentence carefully. The capacity to run and the capacity to withhold the run are the same capacity. That is the bridge to §5.

## 5. Autonomy: the free pole, and where free will actually lives

The naïve picture of freedom is *freedom from the current* — the agent who steps off the conveyor, who stops running. The continuity equation forbids this reading. **You cannot not-run.** Stopping is itself a run toward a terminal state: the prey that halts is eaten; the body that halts starves; the still pool still drains. Setting $\mathbf{j} = 0$ does not exempt you from (2.1); it only changes which term balances $\partial\rho/\partial t$. There is no point on the manifold where *currere* is switched off.

So if autonomy is real, it cannot be located in $\mathbf{j}$. It must be located in $S$.

$$\boxed{\;\text{Autonomy} \;=\; \text{endogenous control of the source term } S.\;}$$

The autonomous agent is the one that *feeds its own current* — that is its own boundary condition. The word says so directly: **auto-nomos**, self-law, self-running. And the framework's own operator has been saying so all along: the condition for an autonomous interior is

$$\dot{\mathcal{V}}_{\text{exo}} = 0, \tag{5.1}$$

the exogenous variety-injection rate vanishes — nothing outside is setting your $S$. By contrast **heteronomy** is $S$ imposed from without: you run, genuinely and fully, but down someone else's gradient, fed from someone else's reservoir. The heteronomous agent is not idle. It is maximally busy. It is simply not the author of its own busyness.

This dissolves the apparent paradox of §3 and §4. The algorithm (exogenous $S$, deterministic) and the president-elect (endogenous $S$, willed) are the same verb because *running* was never the variable that distinguished them. The variable is **who holds the Feed**.

And now the recursive jewel of the cluster. **"Running for"** — president, office, *your life*, *any purpose* — is the teleological *for*, the boundary condition that *sets the gradient* $-\nabla\phi$. To run *for* something is to choose the potential you will run down. "Running for president" is doubly recursive: one runs in order to become *the one who runs the state* — one runs to seize the **national $S$** at L12–L13. And the contest is adjudicated by **currency** (campaign finance, the circulating medium) measured in **current** support (the polls — literally the present running tally). Assemble the sentence and every face of the opening word reappears in it exactly once:

> *A concurrence of konkurrenter circulating currency to capture the executive current.*

That is not wordplay decorating an argument. That is the argument, written in the conjugations of one verb. Agency is **endogenous current-sourcing** — not escape from *currere*, but sovereignty over its $S$.

## 6. Total economy: the failure mode, named exactly

The anti-totalisation commitment of this corpus is non-negotiable and reflexive, so it must be cashed out formally rather than asserted. The continuity equation makes it easy.

A **total economy** — the totalitarian limit, in Arendt's (1951) sense extended to the full manifold — is the regime in which **every source term $S$ across every layer is conscripted to a single sign and a single direction.** $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{j}$ is forced uniform by decree; all runners are made **concurrent** by command; the **konkurrent** — the one who would run against the grain, down a gradient of their own choosing — is abolished. In the symbols of §5, totalitarianism is the **exogenous capture of every Feed**:

$$\dot{\mathcal{V}}_{\text{exo}} > 0 \;\text{ enforced everywhere}, \qquad S_i = S_{\text{state}} \;\; \forall i. \tag{6.1}$$

This is the precise mechanism [DRK-125, *The Totalitarian Sheaf*](https://draken.info/posts/the-totalitarian-sheaf/) describes as the grappling of the skeleton, and it is the forced prevention of the X→Y transition from the TOPO line: to forbid divergence of flow is to forbid anyone from sourcing a current of their own. The tyranny is not that people are made to stop running — they are made to run *harder than ever*, in parade. The tyranny is that **the Feed has been nationalized.**

Anti-totalisation then has an exact statement, and it is not a slogan:

$$\boxed{\;\text{Preserve, for every node, the right to set its own } S.\;}$$

The right to be a *konkurrent* and not merely a *concurrent*. And — symmetrically, from §4 — **the right to not run at all**, because initiation and inhibition are outputs of the *same* control surface (Diamond 2013): the power to start a current *is* the power to withhold it. A regime that can compel the run can also, by the same lever, forbid the rest. *Att löpa eller att inte löpa* is one switch, and freedom is owning the switch.

One reflexive note, per the corpus's own rule. An attentional profile weighted differently on that initiation/inhibition gate — quicker to self-source a current, harder to drive with an imposed one, prone to running hot on its own Feed — is not a *running deficit*. It is a different **$S$-geometry**: more endogenous, less heteronomously steerable. That is a structural fact about where the source sits, and the framework logs it as such and declines the clinical template.

## 7. The cross-linguistic check: why the pun is load-bearing

A skeptic's natural objection: this is a Latin accident. *Currere* happens to be polysemous; build a different theory in a different language and the cluster dissolves.

It does not dissolve. Swedish constructs the **entire** apparatus a second time, on a wholly independent Germanic root — *löpa / lopp / rinna*:

- *löpande* — current, ongoing (*löpande räkning*, a current account — the **currency** sense);
- *löpande band* — the assembly line, **execution-as-conveyance**;
- *förlopp* — the course of events, the running-through: **process**;
- *avlopp* — the drain: **downstream**;
- *lopp* — the race: **konkurrent**;
- *rinna* — to flow: **ström**.

Two etymologically unrelated families — Latin *currere*, Germanic *löpa/rinna* — independently carved the *same joints*: process, current, race, execution, drainage, all built on "run." English, bilingual at the root, **runs** (Germanic) its **currents** (Latin) and feels no seam.

This convergence is the evidence that the mapping is not an etymological coincidence but a **stable attractor in how minds section the concept of carrying-forward-through-time**. When two independent lexical lineages discover the same metaphor — that flowing, processing, competing, and executing are one act of *running* — the parsimonious explanation is not "twice-lucky homonymy." It is that the conceptual structure being named is real and layer-invariant. The pun is load-bearing because the *world* is. *Currere* and *löpa* are two charts on one manifold, and they agree on the overlap — which is, in the formalism of [Hansen & Ghrist (2019)](https://draken.info/posts/the-substrate-and-the-game/), exactly the condition for a global section to exist. The concept *glues*.

## 8. Where this breaks, and how it could be falsified

The corpus does not get to invoke its own falsifiability as a flourish, so here are the seams, honestly marked.

1. **The central claim is linguistically falsifiable, and that is its strength.** The load-bearing assertion is that *deterministic execution* and *autonomous agency* are systematically co-lexified on a "running" root — that "run the algorithm" and "run for office" are not accidents. The clean falsifier is a natural language that lexicalizes these two poles on **systematically unrelated** roots, keeping mechanical execution and willed striving etymologically and metaphorically disjoint across its whole vocabulary. The Latin/Germanic convergence is the supporting datum; a documented counter-family is the refutation. I have checked two branches of one family (Italic, Germanic) and they agree. Two is not many. A Uralic check — Finnish *juosta* (to run) versus *toimeenpanna* (to execute/implement) — is the obvious next probe, and I flag in advance that Finnish may *weaken* the claim, since its execution vocabulary looks more compositional than running-rooted. That is the right kind of exposure to leave open.

2. **The continuity equation is a model, not a proof.** Writing autonomy as $\dot{\mathcal{V}}_{\text{exo}} = 0$ and tyranny as enforced $S_i = S_{\text{state}}$ is a *structural analogy* with real explanatory economy, not a measured law of political physics. It earns its keep if it makes the anti-totalisation principle sharper and the autonomy/heteronomy distinction crisper — which I claim it does — but it does not turn ethics into electrodynamics, and pretending otherwise would be the exact totalizing overreach the post argues against.

3. **"Running for president" is the strongest case and may be flattering the thesis.** The recursive sentence in §5 is almost too clean. Electoral language is unusually saturated with *currere* derivatives in English specifically; the effect may be partly an artifact of English's Latin-Germanic doubling rather than a deep universal. Marked, not leaned on.

4. **Single-stalk caveat — explicit, per protocol.** Everything above is one synthesis from one model. Before any part of it is treated as load-bearing it requires the six-model Clinch. I would route §2 and §6 — the continuity-equation framing, with $S$ as the joint seat of autonomy *and* totalitarianism — past Kimi and DeepSeek first, because that identification is the move doing the real work and the one I am least confident survives an adversarial reading. The cross-linguistic claim in §7 wants a linguist's stalk, not just a second model's.

What survives the caveats is the core, and it is enough to build on: **one verb carries the whole span from the gate that cannot refuse to run to the agent who chooses what to run for; the difference between them is never the running but always the Feed; and the political crime with a name is the capture of the Feed.** The race and the embrace are one morpheme. *Con-currere.* Run with; run against; run.
